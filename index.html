How it works

  • When: the scan runs after every US close (about 00:30 Bahrain time) and lists picks for the next session. Picks only appear on days the whole market dips, so many days have none. That is the strategy working as designed.
  • Entry: a limit buy at the listed price, good for the next session only. If it doesn't fill that day, the trade is cancelled.
  • If it fills at the open below your limit: keep the same dollar distances. Set the stop and target from your actual fill price, using the risk/reward amounts shown.
  • Exit: stop loss or take profit, whichever comes first. If neither is hit after 10 trading days, sell at that day's close.
  • Picks: up to 5 a day per strategy, most oversold first, and never a stock that already has an open trade.

Backtest notes

  • Prices: Yahoo Finance daily bars, about 10 years. Each strategy was designed on 2017–2022 data and checked on 2023 onward ("validation").
  • Grading is conservative. If the stop and target are both touched on one day, it counts as a stop. A target on the fill day doesn't count if the fill was intraday. A gap through the stop exits at the open. Every entry includes 0.05% slippage.
  • Random-pick check, same rules but random stocks (Oct 2026): 1.5:1 trades won about 41% (profit factor 1.03), and 2:1 trades won about 33.5% (profit factor 0.97). The strategies' edge is the gap above those numbers.
  • The universe is today's S&P 500, so stocks that dropped out of the index in the past are missing. This flatters the backtest a little.
  • Past results don't guarantee future results. This page is a mechanical screen, not financial advice.
